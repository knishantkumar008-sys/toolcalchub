USA Federal Income Tax Guide 2026: Brackets, Deductions & Take-Home Pay

📅 March 2026⏱ 9 min read✍️ ToolCalcHub Team

The US federal income tax system uses a progressive bracket system. Understanding how it works helps you plan better and avoid surprises at tax time. Here's the complete 2026 guide.

2026 Federal Tax Brackets (Single Filers)

Taxable IncomeTax Rate
$0 – $12,40010%
$12,401 – $50,40012%
$50,401 – $105,70022%
$105,701 – $200,80024%
$200,801 – $254,90032%
$254,901 – $640,60035%
Over $640,60037%

Standard Deduction 2026

  • Single: $16,100
  • Married Filing Jointly: $32,200
  • Head of Household: $24,150

FICA Taxes (Social Security + Medicare)

Social Security: 6.2% on first $176,100 of wages
Medicare: 1.45% on all wages (no cap)
Additional Medicare: 0.9% on wages above $200,000 (single)
