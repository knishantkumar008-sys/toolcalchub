Privacy Policy Guide 2026: GDPR, CCPA & What Your Website Needs

📅 March 2026⏱ 7 min read✍️ ToolCalcHub Team

If your website collects any data — even just through Google Analytics or AdSense — you legally need a privacy policy. Here's what GDPR, CCPA, and other regulations require in 2026.

Do You Need a Privacy Policy?

Yes, if your website does ANY of the following: uses cookies, has Google Analytics/AdSense, collects email addresses, processes payments, or has user accounts. Virtually every website needs one.

What GDPR Requires (EU/UK)

  • What data you collect and why (legal basis)
  • How long you retain data
  • User rights: access, deletion, portability
  • Contact details for data controller
  • Cookie consent mechanism

What CCPA Requires (California)

  • Categories of personal information collected
  • Purposes for collection
  • Right to opt-out of data sale
  • Right to deletion
