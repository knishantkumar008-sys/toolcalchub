Small Wind Turbines in USA 2026: Federal Tax Credit, Costs & Best Locations

📅 March 2026⏱ 9 min read✍️ ToolCalcHub Team

The USA offers a generous 30% federal tax credit for small wind turbine installations under the Residential Clean Energy Credit. Combined with strong wind resources in many states, residential wind is increasingly viable for rural homeowners.

Federal Tax Credit for Wind 2026

Residential Clean Energy Credit: 30%
Applies to small wind turbines (100 kW or less)
No maximum limit — 30% of total installed cost
Rolls over to future tax years if not fully used

When Does Wind Beat Solar in the USA?

Wind outperforms solar in the Great Plains, Upper Midwest, and coastal areas where wind speeds consistently exceed 6 m/s. In these areas, a wind turbine often achieves 30–40% capacity factor — generating power day and night, year-round.
