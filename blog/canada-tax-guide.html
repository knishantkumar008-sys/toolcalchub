Canada Federal Tax Guide 2026: CPP, EI, and Take-Home Pay Calculator

📅 March 2026⏱ 8 min read✍️ ToolCalcHub Team

Canada's tax system includes federal tax, provincial tax, CPP contributions, and EI premiums. The 2026 federal budget reduced the lowest bracket from 15% to 14% — here's everything you need to know.

Basic Personal Amount (BPA) 2026

The Basic Personal Amount for 2026 is CA$16,129 — this is a non-refundable tax credit that effectively makes the first ~$16,129 of income tax-free.

CPP and EI Contributions 2026

CPP (Canada Pension Plan): 5.95% on earnings $3,500–$74,600
Max CPP contribution: CA$4,226/year
EI (Employment Insurance): 1.64% up to $68,900
Max EI premium: CA$1,130/year

Don't Forget Provincial Tax!

Provincial tax is additional — ranging from about 5% (Alberta has no provincial income tax on lower incomes) to 21% (Quebec). Ontario adds up to 13.16%, BC up to 20.5%.
