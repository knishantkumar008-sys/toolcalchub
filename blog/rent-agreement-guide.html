Rent Agreement Guide 2026: Essential Clauses + Free Template

📅 March 2026⏱ 8 min read✍️ ToolCalcHub Team

A rent agreement (also called a lease agreement or tenancy agreement) is one of the most important legal documents for both landlords and tenants. A poorly written lease can lead to costly disputes. Here's what every rent agreement must include.

Essential Clauses in Every Rent Agreement

  • Parties: Full legal names of landlord and all tenants
  • Property description: Complete address including unit number
  • Rent amount: Monthly rent, due date, accepted payment methods
  • Security deposit: Amount, conditions for deduction, return timeline
  • Lease term: Start and end dates, renewal conditions
  • Maintenance responsibilities: Who handles what repairs
  • Subletting clause: Whether subletting is allowed
  • Termination notice: How much notice either party must give
