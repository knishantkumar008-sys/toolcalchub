Peak Sun Hours by Country: USA, UK, Germany, India, Canada 2026

📅 March 2026⏱ 7 min read✍️ ToolCalcHub Team

Peak sun hours (PSH) is the most important factor in solar system design. It determines how much electricity your panels will actually produce — and it varies dramatically by location. Here's the complete 2026 data for all major countries.

What Are Peak Sun Hours?

One peak sun hour = one hour of sunlight at an intensity of 1,000 W/m² (the standard test condition for solar panels). A location with 5 PSH/day means the total daily solar energy equals 5 hours of full-intensity sunlight.

Important: PSH is NOT the number of daylight hours. A location may have 14 hours of daylight but only 5–6 PSH because sunlight intensity varies throughout the day.
