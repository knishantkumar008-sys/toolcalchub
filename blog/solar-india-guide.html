Solar Panels for Home in India 2026: Cost, Subsidy & ROI Guide

📅 March 2026⏱ 10 min read✍️ ToolCalcHub Team

India is the world's fastest-growing solar market in 2026. With government subsidies, falling panel prices, and rising electricity rates — solar has never made more financial sense for Indian homeowners.

Payback Period in India

With subsidies and India's high peak sun hours (5–6.5 PSH/day), solar payback in India is the fastest globally — typically 3–5 years for residential systems, making it an exceptional investment.

State-wise Additional Subsidies

Many states offer additional subsidies on top of the central PM Surya Ghar Yojana — Gujarat, Maharashtra, Rajasthan, and Karnataka all have their own incentive programs. Check your state DISCOM website for current offers.
